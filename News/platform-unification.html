One Vanguard, One Engine: Windows & Android Unification

We’re bringing the Windows and Android versions of SSIA: Vanguard Chronicles together on RPG Maker MV. This unification gives both platforms a shared engine foundation and helps keep the game’s development and updates aligned.

Our goal is a more consistent Vanguard experience across devices, with one common direction for future improvements. Thank you for standing with SSIA as we continue this work.
